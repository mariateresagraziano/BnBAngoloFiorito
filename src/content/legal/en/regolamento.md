### Check-in

From 3 p.m. to 9 p.m.

Arrivals after or prior to this time must be agreed with the owner at the time of booking. For each extra hour a surcharge of € 5.00 will be requested, subject to room availability.

It is not allowed to bring friends or relatives who are not staying in the B&B into the room.

It is mandatory to present an identity document for registration as required by current legislation (communication to the local police station of the names and period of stay).

The B&B does not issue any tax documents, but only non-tax stamped receipts certifying the amount and details of the stay.

Upon arrival, guests are invited to view the room and its contents and check that it suits their needs.

Three keys will be given: one for the room, one for the entrance door and one for the pedestrian gate. The remote control of the driveway gate will also be given to guests who use the private parking. Loss of keys and remote control will result in a charge of € 150.00.

### Check-out

On the day of departure, the room must be vacated between 7 and 11.00 a.m. Different times must be agreed in advance with the owner (for each hour before 7 a.m. a surcharge of € 10.00 will be requested). Any delays not previously agreed upon will result in an additional night being charged.

Staying in the room after 11 a.m. will result in a surcharge of € 5.00 for each extra hour (subject to room availability).

At check-out, please return the keys directly to the owner who will then check the room and close the doors of the property.

### Cancellation terms

At the time of booking, 100% of the first night or 30% of the total cost of the stay is paid through an instant bank transfer or other payment methods. The balance must be paid at check-in.

In case of cancellations or changes the deposit will not be refunded. In case of no show, the full amount of the stay will be charged. The same rule applies to early departure.

### Rooms

The cleaning of the rooms takes place every other day or daily, on request. Towels are changed every three to four days, while sheets are changed every week. Custom requests must be agreed with the owner. For each extra linen change, we apply a €5 fee per person.

Before leaving the room, remember to turn off the lights, the air conditioner and the fan, and to lock the door to your room.

Furthermore, before leaving the B&B make sure you have locked both the main door and the gate, to safeguard your own and others' safety.

The B&B declines all responsibility for the loss, theft or damage of valuables left in the rooms, if these had not been handed over for safekeeping on arrival.

Please note that as this is a B&B, cooking meals is not allowed.

### Breakfast

Breakfast is available from 7.00 to 10.00 a.m. The use of the kitchen is reserved only for the preparation of breakfast ordered by paying guests. However, we are happy to offer, at any time of the day, a cup of coffee or tea to guests who ask for it.

### Garden

Guests are free to enjoy the relaxation of the garden located at the rear of the house. They may also use the clotheslines to hang bath towels and swimsuits.

### Prohibitions

It is forbidden to throw sanitary towels, hair or other materials other than toilet paper in the toilet.

Smoking is strictly forbidden in all areas of the B&B.

It is forbidden to eat or drink in the room. Guests wishing to have a snack or a packed lunch can use the table in the breakfast room or the tables in the garden.

Avoid loud noises, loud conversations or any behavior that may disturb or harm other guests. Observe total silence from 10 p.m. to 8 a.m. and from 2 p.m. to 4 p.m.

Do not wander into the private parts of the house inhabited by the owners: the front and west sides of the villa. Avoid wearing, outside the rooms, clothes that could offend the modesty of others.

### Compensation for damages

Each guest is required to compensate for damage caused to the structure and for improper use of the fixtures. To open the shutters, since they have a particular closure, it is preferable to ask the owners about how to open them.

As soon as you enter the room, it is advisable to check that everything is working and to report any problems encountered immediately.

Staying at the B&B *Angolo Fiorito* implies acceptance of these rules and regulations. In the event of non-compliance, the guest will be required to leave the facility immediately, without refunds and, if necessary, also with the intervention of the police.

These regulations stem from our intent to offer a high-quality service in a comfortable and relaxing environment where each guest can enjoy their stay to the fullest and in complete tranquility.

### Thank you for choosing B&B *Angolo Fiorito* and we wish you a happy stay!
